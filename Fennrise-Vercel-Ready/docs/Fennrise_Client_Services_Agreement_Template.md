## 3. Scope and exclusions

Fennrise will provide only the work expressly listed in the SOW. Anything not listed is outside scope unless both parties approve a written change request.

Common exclusions unless specifically included: ongoing content writing, photography/video production, paid advertising, third-party subscription fees, domain purchases, hosting renewals, data migration, hardware, regulatory certification, app-store fees and ongoing maintenance after the included support period.

## 4. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate requirements, timely feedback, approvals, brand assets, content, credentials and lawful access to systems needed for the project. The Client confirms it has the right to provide all supplied text, images, logos, data and other materials.

Delays caused by missing Client information, approvals or access may move the delivery schedule.

**Project fee:** INR [amount] plus applicable taxes, if any.  
**Advance:** [amount / percentage] due before work begins.  
**Milestones:** [schedule].  
**Final payment:** due before final production handover unless the SOW states otherwise.

Third-party costs are separate unless expressly included. Fennrise will not knowingly incur material third-party charges on the Client's behalf without approval.

Late or unpaid invoices may pause work or handover after reasonable notice.

## 6. Revisions and change requests

The project includes **[number]** revision rounds for the deliverables identified in the SOW. A revision adjusts agreed work; a new feature, new page, changed workflow, new integration or material change of direction is a change request.

Fennrise will quote or agree the effect of a change request on price and timing before implementing it.

## 7. Delivery and acceptance

Fennrise will provide a review build or staging version before final launch where practical. The Client will review deliverables and report material issues within **[number] business days**.

A deliverable is considered accepted when the Client approves it in writing, launches/uses it in production, or does not report a material scope-related issue within the agreed review period.

## 8. Intellectual property

The Client retains ownership of Client-provided materials.

After full payment, ownership or licence rights in custom project deliverables will be as stated in the SOW. Unless the SOW says otherwise, third-party software, open-source libraries, fonts, APIs, templates, hosting platforms and other licensed components remain subject to their own licences and terms.

Fennrise retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, reusable know-how, generic components, internal processes and technology that were not created exclusively for the Client.

**Portfolio permission:** [Allowed / not allowed / requires written approval].

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will use reasonable care to protect non-public business, technical and commercial information received from the other party and will use it only as needed for the project, except where disclosure is required by law or the information is already lawfully public.

## 10. Data and security

If Fennrise needs access to personal data or production systems, the parties will limit access to what is reasonably necessary for the project. Credentials should be shared through an agreed secure method rather than ordinary messages where practical.

For projects where Fennrise processes meaningful volumes of Client customer/user personal data, the parties should use an additional data-processing schedule covering roles, instructions, security, subprocessors, retention, deletion and incident handling.

The Client is responsible for identifying any industry-specific legal, regulatory or compliance requirements that apply to its business unless Fennrise expressly agrees in writing to provide that compliance work.

## 11. Third-party services

Projects may depend on third-party services such as hosting providers, domain registrars, payment providers, APIs, databases, email services, analytics or app stores. Their availability, pricing, policies and outages are outside Fennrise's control.

The SOW should identify important third-party dependencies and who owns/pays for each account.

## 12. Support and maintenance

Included post-launch support: **[duration and scope]**.

The included support period covers correction of reproducible defects in Fennrise-delivered work that do not result from new requirements, Client changes, third-party changes, expired services, misuse or unsupported environments.

Ongoing maintenance, content updates, new features and third-party changes are separate unless included in the SOW.

Existing clients may contact **support@fennrise.com**.

## 13. Cancellation

Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not corrected within a reasonable written cure period. The Client may also cancel for convenience, but completed work, committed third-party costs and non-refundable milestones remain payable as stated in the SOW.

On termination, Fennrise will provide paid-for completed deliverables that are reasonably capable of handover, subject to outstanding payment and third-party licence restrictions.

## 14. Warranties and disclaimers

Fennrise will perform the agreed services with reasonable care and skill. Except for express commitments in this Agreement/SOW and rights that cannot legally be excluded, no software or online service can be guaranteed to be uninterrupted, completely secure, compatible with every future third-party change or free from every defect.

## 15. Limitation of liability

The project-specific SOW should state the agreed liability cap appropriate to the project value and risk. Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded or limited under applicable law.

Neither party should be liable for indirect or consequential losses to the extent such exclusion is permitted by applicable law, subject to any project-specific exceptions agreed in writing.

## 16. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Subject to mandatory rights or dispute processes under applicable law, disputes will be subject to competent courts in India unless the parties agree another lawful dispute mechanism in writing.

## 17. Notices

General project notices may be sent to the project contacts listed in the SOW. Formal legal notices to Fennrise should be sent to **legal@fennrise.com**.

## 18. Entire agreement and priority

This Agreement, the applicable SOW/quotation/proposal and any signed change requests form the complete written agreement for the project. If they conflict, the signed project-specific SOW or change request controls for that project to the extent of the conflict.
